What changes from Lamha 2

  • Categories, each with its own page, instead of one long page: Lamha 2's sections, in its order. Each setting is a Windows 11 settings card: its name and hint, the control at the end.
  • Writing tools: five providers, with OpenAI and another server (any OpenAI-compatible one: LM Studio, OpenRouter, Groq…). Click one to see its setup.
  • New: the card's background (Acrylic or solid), your data's folder and "delete my data", and the updates and Start with Windows (from Lamha 2's tray and its app settings) under "Lamha".
  • Gone, with Firefox: how the card appears (the floating button), excluded sites, page translation, and the buttons inside text fields. The mistake journal moved to Write; drawing with the graphics card is Electron's.
