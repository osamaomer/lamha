What changes from Lamha 2

  • The window: Windows draws its corners, edge and shadow, with Acrylic behind (or solid, under Appearance).
  • Its height follows the answer, up to 540 px (Lamha 2's limit); then the middle scrolls, and the bar and the links stay.
  • New in the bar: Forward next to Back, and Pin. A pinned card stays open when you click elsewhere, and you can drag it by its bar.
  • Prices in your currency (new in 3.0).
  • Edges line up: the bar, the text and the links all start 16 px from the edge, on both sides and in both languages. Gaps are on the 4 px grid.
  • Everything else is Lamha 2's: the sections, colours, sizes and words.

Questions

  1. Corners: 8 px or 14 px?Recommended: 8 px, drawn by Windows. It's what Acrylic and Windows' shadow need; 14 px (as in Lamha 2) needs a see-through window, without Acrylic and with more memory.
  2. Icons: Lamha 2's, or Windows' (Segoe Fluent Icons)?Recommended: Lamha 2's line icons, as today (drawn here).
  3. Back and Forward: shown as a pair once you've followed a word (Forward dimmed until you go back), so the bar doesn't jump?Recommended: yes.
