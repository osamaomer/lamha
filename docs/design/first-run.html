First run

What a new Lamha 3 opens with, step by step: the first-run window (640 × 540, Mica) at each step. Someone coming from Lamha 2 gets step 2; everyone else goes from 1 to 3. Almost all the wording is new: it's underlined, for you to reword.

How it goes

  • Five steps at most, each one screen: the language, Lamha 2's data (only when it's found), a first lookup, the writing tools (optional), and ready.
  • Learning by doing: step 3 asks you to select a word in a sentence and press the shortcut; the real card opens. Writing tools and the clipboard are named there with their shortcuts.
  • Nothing is chosen for you: no AI until you pick one (it can wait), and clipboard history stays off unless you turn it on (Lamha 2's rule).
  • At the end, the window closes and Lamha waits by the clock.
