03

How it fits Lamha

What building it would mean, in short. The cost estimate is in the conversation; the plan gets it once you approve a design.

  • Most of it exists. The card, the translators, the AI, Review, Listen and side by side are in Core already. The reader adds an EPUB reader and a page to show it on.
  • Native text, no web view. An EPUB is a ZIP of web pages. Core reads it and the page shows it as WinUI text (RichTextBlock): the same reader offline Wikipedia needs.
  • Its own window and process. Closing the book gives all its memory back (WinUI keeps a closed window: decision 0021).
  • Your marks stay on this PC. Highlights, notes and your place are kept in Lamha's database, tied to the book's file.
  • Only books without DRM open: Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks, your own files. Books bought on Kindle or Google Play are locked to their own apps.
  • New in the design system: the paper and black pages, four highlight colours, and icon names for Highlight (E7E6), Note (E70B), Side by side (E89A), Panel (E8A0), Bookmark (E8A4) and Library (E8F1), to be checked on Windows.
04

Questions

Each with a recommendation.

Where does the reader start?

Recommended: a Read page in Lamha's pane, which is the library; a book opens in its own window. The window can then close and give its memory back.

Does one click on a word open the card?

Recommended: yes, as on a Kindle. A drag selects text, and a double-click selects one word to highlight it.

Paper and black pages: a new kind of colour?

Recommended: yes, for the book's page only. They'd be the first colours outside the system's "colour carries meaning" rule.

Scroll or turning pages?

Recommended: scroll first, which is simpler and works with side by side. Pages could come later.

Underline the words you've looked up?

Recommended: on, with a switch in Aa. Meeting a word again and remembering it is the point.

Notes about the time (Michaelmas): from where?

Recommended: from the AI, saved with the book. Offline notes for famous classics could come later.

The name?

"Lamha Reader" and "قارئ لمحة" in this draft, with "Read" for the pane. Yours to choose.
