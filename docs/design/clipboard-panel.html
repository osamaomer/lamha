The clipboard quick panel

Alt+Shift+V in any program: what you copied, to search and paste, with Lamha's tools on a copy. Lamha 2's panel (380 × 460) in each of its states, drawn with the tokens over a desktop so the Acrylic shows.

What changes from Lamha 2

  • The same panel and keys: type to search, ↑ ↓ to choose, Enter pastes, Shift+Enter pastes plain text, Ctrl+1…9 pastes that row, Ctrl+P pins, Delete deletes (with Undo), Tab opens the tools, Esc closes.
  • The card's window: Windows' 8 px corners and shadow, Acrylic behind; it opens under the mouse, and gives the focus back to your program to paste.
  • Tools on a copy: only the ones that fit it (translate, write it in English, proofread, summarize, look up, add to review, paste as plain text), numbered 1–9. An AI result is never pasted without its preview.
  • All the words are Lamha 2's: nothing new to word.
