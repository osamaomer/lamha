What changes from Lamha 2

  • One Windows 11 window: Mica behind, its own title bar, and Windows' navigation on the start side (it folds to icons when the window is narrow). Settings is a page here.
  • Translate keeps Lamha 2's box, Today and the recent lookups, and shows the whole answer, as on the card: the window has the room.
  • Write and Review are Lamha 2's tabs, unchanged: the tools, the corrections, your most frequent mistake; the flashcards, Space and 1 / 2 / 3.
  • History is new as a page: every lookup by day, with search (Lamha 2 showed the last 12).
  • Clipboard is the history with Lamha's tools on a copy: translate, write it in English, summarize, add to review.
