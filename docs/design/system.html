Lamha, built like Windows' Settings

One system for every screen of Lamha 3: Windows 11's own measurements, controls, icons and motion, with Lamha's character kept in the answer, Review and the celebrations. Everything here is drawn at its real size. Words underlined in orange are new wording, waiting for your approval.

09

For your sign-off

Answers turn this draft into the system the code follows (step 2 of the plan).

The bookmark becomes a star?

Segoe Fluent Icons has no bookmark. Windows' own apps (Edge, Photos) save with the star. Recommendation: the star, filled once the word is in Review.

The pane's coloured icons

Six drafts in Icons. Approve, or say which to redraw and how (colour, object, weight).

The accent in dark

Windows fills its main buttons in dark with a light shade of the accent and dark text; Lamha 3.0 uses a deeper indigo with white text. Recommendation: Windows' way (drawn on this page in dark).

The new wording

Everything underlined in orange: the search's placeholder, Rewrite, the InfoBars, the empty state, the paste chip. Reword in either language.

Sign off the system
