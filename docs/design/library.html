The library

The second design of Lamha Reader: a new Read page in Lamha's main window, with the book you're reading, your books, and free books to find and download without leaving Lamha. Click a book to open it, use its ⋯ menu, add a book, drop an EPUB, Word or text file on the window, or open Find books and download one. All of the wording is a draft for you to rewrite.

02

What's on the page

From the top, and what each part is for.

  • Read, a new page in the pane (القارئ in Arabic), between Write and Review: reading feeds Review. Its coloured icon is an open book, drawn like the others (drafts in Windows Settings' style).
  • Two tabs, Your books and Find books, under the page's title, as Windows' SelectorBar: the books you have, and the free ones you can get.
  • Continue reading: the last book you read, where you are, the time left, and Continue, the page's one accent button. "Words from this book" opens Review with them.
  • You know 94% of its words: worked out on this PC from the words in your History and Review, so you can tell how hard a book will be before you start. On Continue reading, in a book's details, and in one short line under every cover.
  • Your books, as covers: the book's own cover when the EPUB has one, or one made from its title in colours chosen from the title, so the same book always looks the same. A Word or text file gets a page-like cover with its name. Under each: how far you've read, New, or Finished, and how many of its words you know.
  • Find, filter and sort: search by title or author; All, Reading, New, Finished; sort by recent, title or author.
  • Each book's ⋯ menu: Open, Mark as finished, Remove from library (with Undo, as everywhere in Lamha). Removing it from the library deletes Lamha's copy, never your original file.
  • Add a book from a file, or drop one on the window: EPUB, DOCX or TXT, or a comic as CBZ or CBR. Lamha keeps its own copy in its data folder, so the book still opens, with its highlights and notes, if the original moves or is deleted; the backup in Settings includes it.
  • Find books: free books from Project Gutenberg and Standard Ebooks, and public-domain comics, with their covers. Search by title or author, pick a category, download with one click: the book lands in Your books, ready to read. For people who don't know where to start, the first view is the most-read books.
03

Questions

The first draft's six, as you answered them on 28 October, and the catalogue's new ones, each with a recommendation.

Where does Read go in the pane?

Answered: between Write and Review, as drawn.

"You know 94% of its words": where?

Answered: on Continue reading, in a book's details, and under every cover, in one short line.

Covers made from a title, when a book has none?

Answered: yes, in colours chosen from the title.

Does Lamha keep its own copy of each book?

Answered: yes, so the book and its highlights and notes stay together.

Free books: links, or a catalogue inside Lamha?

Answered: a catalogue, searched, in categories, downloaded inside Lamha, as a Find books tab of the Read page.

The pane's name in Arabic?

Answered: القارئ.

"You know __%" in Find books, before downloading?

Answered: not at first. Lamha needs the book's words to count them; it shows once the book is downloaded. Later, a small word list per book could come with the catalogue.

Which comics?

Answered: public-domain comics in Find books, from libraries that allow it, checked one by one before they're built in (as drawn: Little Nemo, The Yellow Kid); and your own comics, CBZ or CBR files you've downloaded, added like a book or dropped on the window.

A download while you leave the page?

Answered: it carries on, and Read shows the book when it's ready. Closing Lamha stops it.
