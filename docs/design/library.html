The second design of Lamha Reader: a new Read page in Lamha's main window, with the book you're reading, your books, and where to find free ones. Click a book to open it, use its ⋯ menu, add a book, or drop an EPUB, Word or text file on the window. All of the wording is a draft for you to rewrite.

02

What's on the page

From the top, and what each part is for.

  • Read, a new page in the pane, between Write and Review: reading feeds Review. Its coloured icon is an open book, drawn like the others (drafts in Windows Settings' style).
  • Continue reading: the last book you read, where you are, the time left, and Continue, the page's one accent button. "Words from this book" opens Review with them.
  • You know 94% of its words: worked out on this PC from the words in your History and Review, so you can tell how hard a book will be before you start. Shown here and in a book's details.
  • Your books, as covers: the book's own cover when the EPUB has one, or one made from its title, as drawn. A Word or text file gets a page-like cover with its name. Under each: how far you've read, New, or Finished.
  • Find, filter and sort: search by title or author; All, Reading, New, Finished; sort by recent, title or author.
  • Each book's ⋯ menu: Open, Mark as finished, Remove from library (with Undo, as everywhere in Lamha). Removing it from the library deletes Lamha's copy, never your original file.
  • Add a book from a file, or drop one on the window: EPUB, DOCX or TXT. Lamha keeps its own copy in its data folder, so the book stays if the original moves.
  • Free books to read: Project Gutenberg and Standard Ebooks, opened in the browser. Download an EPUB there, then add it here.
03

Questions

Each with a recommendation.

Where does Read go in the pane?

Recommended: between Write and Review, as drawn. Reading leads to Review.

"You know 94% of its words": show it?

Recommended: yes, on Continue reading and in a book's details, not on every cover, where it would crowd the grid.

Covers made from a title, when a book has none?

Recommended: yes, as drawn, in a few colours chosen from the title so the same book always looks the same.

Does Lamha keep its own copy of each book?

Recommended: yes, in Lamha's data folder, so moving or deleting the original doesn't lose the book or its highlights. "A copy of my data" in Settings would then include them.

Free books: links, or a catalogue inside Lamha?

Recommended: links now. Standard Ebooks and Project Gutenberg both publish catalogues Lamha could browse and download from later (an idea for after the reader works).

The pane's name in Arabic?

"قراءة" in this draft, as the other pages are nouns (ترجمة، كتابة، مراجعة). Yours to choose.
